Club Rules

Respect the Team

Treat teammates, coaches, referees, and opponents with respect. A positive attitude helps create a better environment for everyone.

Show Up On Time

Please arrive a little early for training sessions and matches. Being prepared helps the whole team stay organized.

Stay Committed

Regular attendance is important. If you cannot attend, let a coach or team leader know beforehand.

Play Fair

Football is competitive, but fair play always comes first. We expect players to compete with integrity and sportsmanship.

Football Basics

The Goal

The objective of football is simple: score more goals than your opponent before the match ends.

Players on the Pitch

Each team has eleven players, including a goalkeeper. Teamwork is key to success.

Match Length

A standard match lasts 90 minutes, split into two halves with a short break in between.

Fouls

Actions like pushing, tripping, holding, or dangerous tackles may result in free kicks, penalties, or disciplinary action.

Yellow Cards

A yellow card serves as a warning for unsporting behavior or repeated rule violations.

Red Cards

Serious misconduct can result in a red card, meaning the player must leave the field immediately.

What to Wear

Club Jersey 👕

Wear the official AngryBirds FC jersey during matches and club events.

Football Shorts 🩳

Comfortable sports shorts that allow free movement during training and games.

Socks and Shin Guards 🧦 🛡️

Shin guards are required for safety and must be covered by football socks.

Football Boots 👟

Wear appropriate boots for the playing surface to ensure comfort and grip.

Before You Step Onto the Pitch

  • Jewelry such as rings, watches, or necklaces should be removed.
  • Sharp accessories or items that could cause injury are not allowed.
  • Damaged footwear should not be used during training or matches.
  • Players are expected to follow the club's code of conduct both on and off the field.

Rules and Attire

Whether you're joining your first training session or preparing for match day, these guidelines will help you represent AngryBirds FC with confidence and pride.
