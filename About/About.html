About Our Club

Founded in 2015, Angry Birds FC is a football club dedicated to excellence, teamwork, and player development. We strive to create opportunities for talented students while fostering a strong connection with our supporters and our university community.

Club History

Our journey began in 2015 with a small group of passionate footballers. Over the years, the club has grown into a respected organization known for competitive success and commitment to player development.

Mission and Vision

Our Mission

To develop talented students around our campus and inspire students through football.

Our Vision

To become one of the leading university football clubs and a source of pride for our supporters.
